Solve a network

Choose a network and find the shortest tour that walks every street, or only some of them, with one postman or several. The solver runs inside your browser: nothing is sent to a server, except street data requested from OpenStreetMap.

Network

Classical arc-routing benchmarks; see the 2026 report, chapter 7.
First line: junctions. Second: streets. Then one line per street: from to cost. Up to 2,500 junctions; for larger networks use the Python package.
Pan and zoom the map to a few blocks (up to about 1 km², or 1,500 junctions), then fetch the streets. One-way streets are kept.

Service

Every postman starts and ends at the depot (the square). With several, the longest route is made as short as possible.
