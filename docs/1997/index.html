The 1997 report

Resolución del problema del cartero chino (CPP) mediante la aplicación de un proceso de branch and bound. Miquel Monserrat Pons, final-year project at the Escola Tècnica Superior d'Enginyeria Industrial de Barcelona (ETSEIB), supervised by Ramón Companys Pascual, June 1997.

The report is in Spanish. It introduces graphs, matchings and Eulerian circuits, solves the matching step of the Chinese Postman Problem with a purpose-built branch-and-bound search, and studies its behaviour on three districts of Vilanova i la Geltrú. The programs, written in Turbo C, are listed in full in Annex 1. The text is searchable in the viewer below.
